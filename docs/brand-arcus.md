# Arcus: Brand Identity

> Status: final brand direction. Arcus leads with The Arc. Personality is a trajectory, not a type. This document specifies the core narrative, positioning, voice, naming, taglines, and visual directions for the platform.

---

## 1. The chosen direction: The Arc

Arcus is the arc of becoming. Personality is not a position; it is a trajectory. Every result the system produces includes where the user is headed, not just where they stand. The brand's entire identity flows from that single premise.

### Why The Arc is the strongest fit

Three reasons, each grounded in what the system actually measures.

**The system was built for trajectory.** Every test has a direction component. SCA measures waxing and waning. MMA measures the gap between stated and observed cognition, which is a trajectory between two states of self-knowledge. SSA measures commitment, which is the trajectory of identity from provisional to claimed. PTA measures stance, which is the direction of one's relationship with time. No other brand direction connects to all four tests the way trajectory does.

**Trajectory is the user-facing insight.** When someone takes a personality test, the most memorable result is not "you scored 72 on extraversion." It is "your energy is at its peak and you can feel it starting to turn." The trajectory layer makes every result feel alive and personal, because it tells the user something about where they are going, not just where they are. That is the insight worth branding.

**"Arc" is the only interpretation that works as a noun and a verb.** You can see your arc (noun). You arc toward something (verb). The word is active without being aggressive. It is specific without being clinical. It is warm without being generic. It contains the brand name (Arcus) and the core promise (you are the arc) in the same three letters.

## 2. The core narrative

Arcus exists because personality is not a snapshot. It is a curve.

Most personality tests take a photograph: freeze you in place, label the frame, hand it back. Arcus takes a different measurement. It asks not just where you are, but which way you are turning. It asks not just what you say about yourself, but whether your choices confirm it. It asks not just how you see yourself, but how you are seen. It asks not just where your attention sits, but how you feel about the passage that carries it.

Every Arcus result has two parts: a position and a direction. The position tells you where you stand. The direction tells you where you are headed. Together, they trace an arc.

Four tests give you four arcs. Your energy arcs through the cycle of the year. Your mind arcs between what you claim and what you do. Your self arcs from who you were to who you are claiming. Your time arcs across the zones you inhabit and the stance you hold toward the whole passage.

The arcs do not always agree. Sometimes your stated self and your observed self point different ways. Sometimes your energy is high but you feel it turning. Sometimes you engage deeply with the future but feel time as a weight. Those tensions are not contradictions to resolve. They are the shape of who you are. The arc is never a straight line.

Arcus does not tell you what you are. It shows you the arc you are on. What you do with it is yours.

## 3. Positioning

### Core promise

**Arcus does not tell you what you are. It shows you the arc you are on.**

This promise holds because the system was designed for it. Every test measures direction and tension, not just position. The promise is not aspirational marketing. It describes what the product does.

### Positioning statement

Arcus is the personality suite that measures the arc of who you are, not just where you stand. Each of four tests maps a different dimension of self (energy, mind, identity, time) and reveals the direction you are heading within it. The result is not a label. It is a point on a curve, with a direction.

### What Arcus is NOT

Arcus is not MBTI. MBTI assigns four binary types and forces people into 16 boxes. Arcus measures continuous dimensions and reports trajectories.

Arcus is not Big Five. Big Five measures five traits and reports percentile scores. Arcus measures four domains with different methodologies and reads the gaps between paired measurements.

Arcus is not a quiz. Quizzes give you a label and a shareable image. Arcus gives you a position, a direction, and the gap between them, and invites you to sit with the tension.

Arcus is not a clinical assessment. Arcus measures the affirming range of each construct and cannot diagnose. It is for reflection and play.

### What Arcus IS

Arcus is the only consumer personality product that:
1. Measures four genuinely different domains (affect, cognition, identity, time) with four genuinely different methodologies.
2. Reads the internal gap in each test (position vs trajectory, stated vs observed, self vs other, engagement vs stance) as an interpretive layer.
3. Treats personality as a trajectory, not a type.
4. Grounds every claim in full-text-verified research and labels every synthesis as unvalidated.

## 4. What this direction excludes

Choosing The Arc means the brand does NOT lead with:
- The gap or tension as a headline concept (too complex for first encounter; it lives in the product experience, discovered through use)
- The four-test completeness argument as the primary voice (important for the platform page, not the brand voice)
- Tension, bow, or contradiction as a primary metaphor (uncomfortable for a consumer context; the arc subsumes tension as curvature)

The gap is still the product's differentiator. But it is discovered through the experience, not announced in the tagline. The user takes a test, sees their position, then discovers their direction and their gap, and the brand name retroactively makes sense: the arc was the curve between those two points all along.

## 5. Taglines

### Primary brand tagline

**You are the arc.**

Three words. Contains the brand name. States the core premise without explaining it. Open-ended. Inviting. The user supplies the meaning: my arc of what? Energy, mind, self, time. All of them.

### Alternatives for specific contexts

- **Not who you are. Where you're going.** Direct rejection of type-based testing. Better for a first encounter with the brand where the user needs to understand what makes Arcus different.
- **Every self is in motion.** The most inclusive framing. Does not assume the user wants to go anywhere specific. Honors the fact that some arcs are slow, some are fast, some are turning back. Motion, not progress.
- **See the arc you're on.** Call to action. Inviting rather than declarative. Works as a button, a headline, or a social prompt.

### Suite-level tagline

**Four arcs. One self.**

Reframes the four-test structure as four perspectives on the same trajectory. Avoids clinical "dimensions" language. Keeps the brand metaphor.

### Test-specific taglines

- Arcus: Solstice. *Which way does your light turn?*
- Arcus: Turing. *Does your mind do what it says?*
- Arcus: Pride. *How well do you know and show yourself?*
- Arcus: Passage. *Where does your time live?*

### Result-ending refrains

Every Arcus result ends with an open trajectory, not a closed label. These are the signature closing lines:

- **You are here. Where are you going?**
- **Arcs move.**
- **This is your arc right now.**

## 7. Narrative voice

### How Arcus speaks

Arcus speaks in the second person, present tense, with motion verbs.

- Never: "You are a Summer." (static label)
- Instead: "Your energy sits at Summer, and it is waxing." (position + direction)

- Never: "You are a Logician." (closed identity)
- Instead: "You say you enjoy thinking, and your choices confirm it." (stated + observed, open)

- Never: "You are future-oriented." (single dimension)
- Instead: "Your future engagement is high, and you carry time as a resource." (engagement + stance)

The voice assumes the user is in motion. It speaks to the direction, not the destination. It surfaces the gap without resolving it. It ends with an open question, not a conclusion.

### Tone

Warm but precise. The voice uses plain English (direction, where you put your energy, how you feel about time) that translates the research language (trajectory, engagement, stance, congruence). It never sounds clinical. It never sounds like a quiz. It sounds like a thoughtful observer who has measured something about you and is showing you the shape of it.

### Result endings

Every Arcus result ends with an open trajectory, not a closed label. The open ending is the brand's signature. It reinforces the core premise: you are not a type. You are a trajectory.

## 8. Visual directions

### Primary visual: the arc with direction

A curved line with a point on it and an arrow showing direction. The point is where you are now. The arrow is your trajectory. The curve is the cycle or plane you are part of.

This visual works for all four tests:
- Solstice: the point sits on a circular wheel (the circumplex), the arrow shows waxing/waning.
- Turing: the point sits on a 2D plane (NFC x FI), with a secondary marker for observed strategy and a line between them (the gap).
- Pride: the point sits on a 2D plane (clarity x authenticity), with an optional second point (informant view) and the distance between them (visibility gap).
- Passage: the point sits in a 3-factor space (past/present/future), with a stance color overlay.

### Secondary visual: the spectrum

Four bands of color, one per test, arranged as an arc. The user's profile is the intensity pattern across the four bands. This visual sells the suite: you can see at a glance that you are high on three dimensions and low on one, or that your results cluster in one trait neighborhood.

### Relationship between the visuals

The arc and the spectrum are not competing visual systems. They are the same shape seen from two angles:
- The **arc** is the curve itself (trajectory).
- The **spectrum** is the arc filled with color (the four domains).

## 9. Launch sequence narrative

For a user encountering Arcus for the first time:

1. **The promise:** "You are the arc." The user understands immediately that Arcus sees them as in motion, not as a fixed type.

2. **The structure:** "Four arcs. One self." The user learns that Arcus is a suite, not a single test, and that each test measures something the others do not.

3. **The discovery:** The user takes a test and discovers that the result is not just a score but a direction: their energy is waxing, or their stated self differs from their observed self, or their identity is claimed or still exploring. The direction is the surprise.

4. **The arc:** "You are here. Where are you going?" The user finishes a test with a sense of direction, not a label. They know where they stand and which way they are turning.

5. **The full picture:** After taking all four, the user sees their four arcs together: energy, mind, self, and time, each with its own direction and its own internal tension. The synthesis is the payoff.

This sequence mirrors the actual product experience: the user discovers the direction mechanic in their first test, then deepens it across the suite.

---

## 10. What the brand does NOT do

- **Does not promise transformation.** Arcus does not say "discover your true self" or "unlock your potential." It says "see the arc you are on." The product measures and reflects. It does not prescribe.

- **Does not promise compatibility.** Arcus does not match you with partners, careers, or friends. It shows you your profile. What you do with it is yours.

- **Does not promise clinical insight.** Arcus measures the affirming range of each construct and cannot detect maladaptive patterns. The scope limit is stated on every result.

- **Does not close.** Arcus never says "you are X, and that is final." Every result is a snapshot of a trajectory. Arcs move. The brand honors that by leaving every result open.
