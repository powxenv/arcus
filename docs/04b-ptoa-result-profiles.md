# PTA Result Profiles

> Companion to `04-passage-of-time-assessment.md`.
> Profiles for all eight PTA temporal types, using an 11-section schema adapted to the three-factor structure (the full 14-section schema from SCA/MMA/SSA is reduced because independent factors do not produce the same quadrant-dynamic structure; "The Complementary Mode" becomes "The Counterweight Type").
> These are the deterministic fallback narratives. The Gemini layer enriches the Archetype and Reflection sections from the player's answer pattern. It never overrides the structured sections.
> Honesty note: profiles describe tendencies associated with a pattern of temporal engagement. They are affirming, non-clinical, and avoid deficit framing. The affirming scope limit (00 §3) applies: these profiles describe the affirming surface of each pattern and cannot detect rumination, hopelessness, or dread. v0.23: the type gains a temporal-stance modifier (resource/weight/gift/mystery) from the added stance layer, grounded in Carstensen (2021).

---

## Canonical schema (every type)

1. **Essence.** One-line core plus the three factor scores.
2. **The Archetype.** Two short narrative paragraphs.
3. **Core Engagement.** What this pattern is oriented toward.
4. **Strengths.**
5. **Blind Spots.** Honest, non-shaming.
6. **Growth Edges.**
7. **In Relationships.** What they offer, need, and risk.
8. **Work Style.** Ideal conditions and pitfalls.
9. **Under Stress.** Stress signature and recovery.
10. **The Counterweight Type.** The type with the opposite engagement pattern, as a learning edge.
11. **Your Reflection.** The poetic closing line.

## 📜 The Keeper  (Past engaged)

**Essence.** You carry your roots as a living thing. The past is where you draw meaning, and it grounds everything else.

### The Archetype

You move through time rooted in where you have been. Memory is not decoration for you; it is ground. The people and places that shaped you, the traditions you inherited, the moments that made you, all of it is alive in how you live now. You honor what came before, and that honoring is a quiet form of love.

You are not stuck in the past. You are fed by it. There is a difference, and it matters. A Keeper who is healthy draws strength from roots and lets that strength carry them forward. The past is your well, not your cage.

### Core Engagement
- Roots, lineage, memory, heritage.
- The thread of how you became who you are.
- Honoring what came before.

### Strengths
- Depth and continuity. You carry meaning across time.
- Loyalty to people, places, and commitments.
- A long view that sees patterns and context.
- The ability to hold a family, a project, a culture together across generations.

### Blind Spots
- Holding on past the point of usefulness.
- Reading the present and future through the past and missing what is genuinely new.
- Equating change with loss.
- Curating memory until it is tidier than the living was.

### Growth Edges
- Let some chapters close. Release is not betrayal.
- Visit the present and the future on purpose. They are not threats to your roots.
- Meet The Wayfinder's forward pull. Roots and horizons can coexist.

### In Relationships
- You offer loyalty, memory, and the feeling of being held across time.
- You may hold on to bonds that have run their course.
- Growth: let people change within the relationship.

### Work Style
- Thrive in stewardship, depth, and continuity: archives, history, conservation, mentoring, family business, restoration.
- Risk: resisting necessary change.
- Ideal: environments that value the long arc.

### Under Stress
- Signature: retreat to the familiar. Seek the known and the preserved.
- Recovery: return to the throughline, but ask what the next chapter needs.

### The Counterweight Type: 🧭 The Wayfinder
Your wholeness lives ahead of you. The Wayfinder's forward pull is the motion your rootedness needs. Roots without horizons can become a closed circle. The Wayfinder shows you that the past is richest when it feeds a future you are walking toward.

### Your Reflection
"You are the keeper of the thread, the one who remembers and honors. Your gift is continuity, and your growth is letting the story keep writing itself forward."

## ⏳ The Witness  (Present engaged)

**Essence.** You live where the past and future resolve into now. The present is rich, and it is enough.

### The Archetype

You are here. Not anchored in what was, not chasing what will be, but genuinely present in the only moment anyone ever actually inhabits. The ordinary is not ordinary to you. A conversation, a meal, the light through a window, all of it lands fully because you are there for it.

This is the active, meaningful presence the time-perspective literature spent decades missing (Vowinckel et al. 2015 built the scale for exactly this). It is not hedonism, which grabs the present for kicks. It is not fatalism, which endures the present because nothing else is possible. It is engagement. You meet the moment, and the moment meets you back.

### Core Engagement
- The current moment, fully met.
- Flow, attention, appreciation of the ordinary.
- Genuine presence with people and tasks.

### Strengths
- Aliveness. You are not elsewhere.
- Attention as a gift to the people and work in front of you.
- Resilience through presence; you handle difficulty by meeting it rather than fleeing it.
- The capacity for joy in small things.

### Blind Spots
- Missing long arcs because the present is so vivid.
- Under-preparing for the future or under-honoring the past.
- Drift, if presence becomes avoidance of direction or memory.
- Others may read you as unambitious or rootless.

### Growth Edges
- Visit the past and the future on purpose. Presence is richer when it includes them consciously.
- Let presence serve something larger than the moment.

### In Relationships
- You offer full attention, which is rare and felt.
- You may resist long-term planning with partners.
- Growth: presence plus direction. Be here, and be going somewhere together.

### Work Style
- Thrive in roles rewarding attention and engagement: craft, care, teaching, performance, hands-on work, therapy.
- Risk: difficulty with long-horizon planning.
- Ideal: environments that value quality of attention over throughput.

### Under Stress
- Signature: narrow to the immediate. Handle what is in front of you.
- Risk: losing the bigger picture entirely.
- Recovery: presence is your strength, but pair it with one forward-looking anchor.

### The Counterweight Type: 🌉 The Bridge
Your wholeness lives in the long arc. The Bridge's span from roots to horizons is the context your presence needs. Presence without context can become drift. The Bridge shows you that being fully here is deepest when you know what came before and what you are walking toward.

### Your Reflection
"You are the one who meets the moment fully, for whom the ordinary is alive. Your gift is presence, and your growth is letting that presence carry a direction."

## 🧭 The Wayfinder  (Future engaged)

**Essence.** The horizon pulls you. The future is alive with possibility, and you walk toward it.

### The Archetype

You move through time forward-facing. There is something ahead of you, and you can feel it. Goals, dreams, the person you are becoming, the thing you are building, all of it pulls you forward and gives the present its shape. You are not waiting for life to happen. You are walking to meet it.

This is not restlessness, though it can look like it from outside. It is orientation. A Wayfinder who is healthy has a direction that makes the present meaningful, not a hunger that makes the present insufficient. The future is your compass, and compasses are for traveling, not for fleeing.

### Core Engagement
- Goals, dreams, the person you are becoming.
- Possibility, what could be.
- Direction and trajectory.

### Strengths
- Vision and follow-through. You close the distance between now and next.
- Resilience grounded in direction.
- The ability to make plans real.
- Trustworthiness on long horizons.

### Blind Spots
- Living in the waiting room of the future; the present becomes a means, not a place.
- Over-planning. Some of the best things cannot be scheduled.
- Mistrusting people who live in the past or the present.
- Confusing the plan with the purpose.

### Growth Edges
- Let the present be a place to live, not only a step on the way.
- Leave room to be surprised by what you did not plan.
- Meet The Keeper's rootedness. Direction is deeper when it grows from somewhere.

### In Relationships
- You offer direction and the sense that the relationship is going somewhere.
- You may under-attend to what is here now.
- Growth: be present in the relationship, not only future-oriented with it.

### Work Style
- Thrive in long-horizon roles: strategy, leadership, research programs, building, medicine, engineering.
- Risk: rigidity when the plan no longer serves.
- Ideal: environments that reward direction and patience.

### Under Stress
- Signature: grip the plan harder. Tighten the timeline, push forward.
- Risk: losing the present entirely.
- Recovery: return to purpose, then ask whether the plan still serves it.

### The Counterweight Type: 📜 The Keeper
Your wholeness lives behind you. The Keeper's rootedness is the ground your trajectory needs. Direction without roots can become flight. The Keeper shows you that the future you are walking toward is deeper when you carry your history with you instead of leaving it.

### Your Reflection
"You are the one who walks toward the horizon and carries a sense of where you are going. Your gift is direction, and your growth is arriving without losing the road."

## 🕯️ The Hearth  (Past + Present engaged)

**Essence.** You carry your roots into the living moment. Memory warms the now, and the now gives memory a place to land.

### The Archetype

You live at the meeting of memory and presence. The past is alive in you, and the present is where you keep it alive. A conversation carries the weight of every conversation before it; a meal carries the warmth of every meal that shaped you. You do not choose between roots and the moment. You bring them together.

This is a generative pattern. Your roots are not a museum; they are fuel for how you live now. And your presence is not rootless; it is fed by everything that brought you here. People feel held by you, because to be with you is to be with your history and your attention at once.

### Core Engagement
- Roots expressed in the living present.
- Tradition carried forward through attention.
- Memory as fuel, not artifact.

### Strengths
- Warmth. You bring depth to ordinary moments.
- Continuity that is alive, not preserved under glass.
- The ability to make others feel they belong to something larger than the moment.
- Gratitude as a daily practice.

### Blind Spots
- Letting the past color the present more than the present deserves.
- Resisting the future because roots and now feel sufficient.
- Nostalgia that drifts toward withdrawal.

### Growth Edges
- Make room for the future. Two lit rooms are good; three can be richer.
- Meet The Flow's forward-and-present motion. You can be rooted, present, and moving.

### In Relationships
- You offer warmth, belonging, and depth.
- You may resist planning the future with partners.
- Growth: carry the past and present into a shared future.

### Work Style
- Thrive in roles connecting past and present: teaching, mentoring, craft with tradition, care work, ministry, family roles.
- Risk: insufficient forward planning.
- Ideal: environments that honor continuity and attention.

### Under Stress
- Signature: deepen into roots and routine. Find the known and the meaningful.
- Risk: withdrawal into memory.
- Recovery: roots are your ground; add one forward-facing commitment.

### The Counterweight Type: 🧭 The Wayfinder
Your wholeness lives ahead. The Wayfinder's future-orientation is the direction your warmth needs. Hearth without horizon can become a closed circle. The Wayfinder shows you that memory and presence are richest when they are walking toward something.

### Your Reflection
"You are the one who keeps the past alive by living it now. Your gift is warmth, and your growth is carrying that warmth forward."

## 🌉 The Bridge  (Past + Future engaged)

**Essence.** You span from roots to horizons. The present is your crossing, not your home.

### The Archetype

You hold the long arc. Where you came from and where you are going are both vividly real to you, and your life is the span between them. You carry your history forward with intention, and you build toward a future that honors it. The present, for you, is mostly the work of building that bridge.

This is a powerful pattern for purpose and legacy. You are the person who inherits something and passes it on changed and strengthened. The risk is that the present becomes a work zone, a means to a future end, lived through rather than lived in. Bridges are for crossing, but you are the one standing on them.

### Core Engagement
- Legacy, continuity across the whole arc.
- Carrying roots into a built future.
- The work of spanning.

### Strengths
- A sense of purpose that draws on history and projects into the future.
- The ability to build something that lasts.
- Depth and direction at once.
- Trustworthiness on generational timescales.

### Blind Spots
- Neglecting the present. The crossing becomes the whole life.
- Treating now as a means to later.
- Holding the past and future so tightly that the moment slips through.

### Growth Edges
- Stop on the bridge sometimes. The view from here is its own thing.
- Meet The Witness's presence. You can carry the arc and still live in the moment.

### In Relationships
- You offer purpose, depth, and the sense of a shared arc.
- You may be so oriented to past and future that the present of the relationship is under-attended.
- Growth: be here, in this moment of the relationship, not only in its history or its trajectory.

### Work Style
- Thrive in legacy and arc-building roles: institution-building, multi-generational craft, family business, long research programs, ministry.
- Risk: living in the work zone, never arriving.
- Ideal: environments that reward building across decades.

### Under Stress
- Signature: grip the arc. Re-examine roots, re-plan the future.
- Risk: the present disappears entirely.
- Recovery: the arc is your strength; add one present-moment anchor.

### The Counterweight Type: ⏳ The Witness
Your wholeness lives in the moment you keep crossing. The Witness's presence is the rest your arc needs. A bridge lived on without rest becomes a treadmill. The Witness shows you that the present is not only the work of spanning; it is where the span is lived.

### Your Reflection
"You are the one who carries the past into the future, for whom life is a long and intentional arc. Your gift is legacy, and your growth is standing still long enough to be where you are."

## 🌊 The Flow  (Present + Future engaged)

**Essence.** You move forward, fully present. Now and next are one motion for you.

### The Archetype

You live in the current where presence meets direction. You are fully engaged with what is in front of you, and what is in front of you is always becoming what comes next. You do not experience the present and the future as separate rooms. You experience them as flow. You are here, and you are going somewhere, and those are the same act.

This is a generative, alive pattern. You bring attention to your work and your direction to your attention. The risk is that you move so fluidly forward that you leave roots behind, unexamined. Flow that never looks back can become a kind of elegant rootlessness.

### Core Engagement
- Engaged presence in service of direction.
- Flow states that move toward goals.
- The fusion of now and next.

### Strengths
- Aliveness plus momentum.
- The ability to be fully in the work while moving it forward.
- Adaptive presence: you meet what arises while keeping your heading.
- Energy that others feel and follow.

### Blind Spots
- Leaving the past unexamined. Roots can look like drag to you.
- Moving so fluidly that you do not integrate what you have passed through.
- Others may feel they cannot keep up, or that you have already left them.

### Growth Edges
- Look back sometimes. The flow is deeper when you know the river's source.
- Meet The Hearth's rooted presence. You can flow and be grounded.

### In Relationships
- You offer aliveness, engagement, and momentum.
- Partners may feel you are always already slightly ahead.
- Growth: be present to the history of the relationship, not only its trajectory.

### Work Style
- Thrive in roles rewarding engaged momentum: founding, building, creative production, performance, urgent or evolving work.
- Risk: leaving a trail without integration.
- Ideal: environments that reward presence and initiative together.

### Under Stress
- Signature: flow faster. Engage harder, move more.
- Risk: fragmenting across too much forward motion.
- Recovery: flow is your strength; add one backward-looking anchor.

### The Counterweight Type: 📜 The Keeper
Your wholeness lives behind you. The Keeper's rootedness is the ground your flow needs. Flow without source can become scattering. The Keeper shows you that the forward motion is deeper when you know, and honor, where it began.

### Your Reflection
"You are the one who moves forward fully present, for whom now and next are one current. Your gift is engaged momentum, and your growth is remembering the source of the river."

## ⚖️ Balanced Time Perspective  (all three engaged)

**Essence.** All three rooms are lit. Past, present, and future are each alive in you, in proportion.

### The Archetype

You hold the whole of time. Your roots are real to you, your present is alive, and your future pulls you, and none of them crowds out the others. This is the pattern the time-perspective literature calls balanced time perspective (Zimbardo & Boyd 1999; Sobol-Kwapinska & Jankowski 2015), and it is the most consistently associated with well-being of any temporal profile.

Balance is not blandness. It is the capacity to draw on the past for meaning, the present for aliveness, and the future for direction, and to move fluidly between them as the moment requires. You can grieve a memory and plan a year and be fully in this conversation, sometimes in the same hour. That flexibility is the gift.

### Core Engagement
- Fluid movement across all three zones.
- Drawing on each as the situation calls for it.
- Integration rather than preference.

### Strengths
- The well-being-associated profile in the time-perspective literature.
- Flexibility: meet memory, presence, and planning as needed.
- Depth, aliveness, and direction in proportion.
- Resilience: you have multiple temporal resources to draw on.

### Blind Spots
- None of the three zones is maximally developed; you may envy the intensity of single-zone types.
- Balance can drift into diffusion if no zone is strongly held.
- The effort of holding all three can be invisible to others.

### Growth Edges
- Recognize that balance is itself a practice, not a default. Tend it.
- Let one zone lead when the moment calls for it; balance is not neutrality.

### In Relationships
- You offer presence, memory, and direction in proportion.
- You can meet partners wherever they are temporally.
- Growth: name which zone a given moment needs, and lead with it.

### Work Style
- Thrive across a wide range of roles; balance is broadly adaptive.
- Risk: none-zone-maximized; may under-perform specialists in narrow contexts.
- Ideal: environments that value integration and flexibility.

### Under Stress
- Signature: hold all three zones; resist collapsing into one.
- Risk: the effort of balance under load.
- Recovery: balance is your practice; let one zone carry you while you recover the others.

### The Counterweight Type: 🍃 The Wanderer
Your wholeness lives in what you hold. The Wanderer's unbound freedom is the release your balance sometimes needs. Holding all three zones is a gift; occasionally letting them all go, in rest or play, is what sustains the holding.

### Your Reflection
"You are the one who holds the whole of time in proportion. Your gift is balance, and your growth is knowing it is a practice, not a resting state."

## 🍃 The Wanderer  (no zone strongly engaged)

**Essence.** You are not strongly pulled by roots, presence, or horizons. You move through time lightly, unbound by any zone.

### The Archetype

You do not strongly lean into the past, the present, or the future. Time, for you, is something you move through without any one zone holding you. This can read, from inside, as freedom: you are not anchored, not driven, not consumed by the moment. You float.

The honest framing matters here. Sometimes this pattern is genuine lightness, a person who has loosened their grip on time and meets it without grasping. And sometimes, the same scores reflect disengagement that is worth paying attention to: low positive engagement across all three temporal zones does correlate with lower well-being in the literature. This assessment cannot tell those apart. It can only describe the surface honestly and flag the underneath.

### Core Engagement
- Movement without strong temporal anchor.
- Freedom from being held by any one zone.
- (Surface) lightness; (possible underneath) disengagement.

### Strengths
- Freedom from being ruled by memory, urgency, or anxiety about the future.
- Adaptability: with no strong temporal grip, you can meet situations as they come.
- A kind of lightness others may find relieving.

### Blind Spots
- Without roots, presence, or direction, life can drift.
- The same pattern that reads as freedom can mask disengagement, low mood, or burnout.
- Others may find it hard to locate you, temporally or otherwise.

### Growth Edges
- If this pattern is genuine lightness, tend it; rest and play sustain it.
- If it feels like flatness or disconnection, that is worth attending to. Consider whether a counselor or trusted person could help you look at it. This assessment cannot tell, and it will not pretend to.
- Try lighting one zone on purpose, even briefly, and notice what shifts.

### In Relationships
- You offer non-attachment and flexibility.
- Partners may feel they cannot find you, temporally or emotionally.
- Growth: name what you are feeling and where you are oriented, even if the answer is "nowhere in particular."

### Work Style
- You can work across contexts without strong temporal preferences getting in the way.
- Risk: drift without direction or engagement.
- Ideal: environments that do not require strong temporal orientation, or that actively value flexibility.

### Under Stress
- Signature: float further. Disengage more.
- Risk: the floating becomes the problem rather than the relief.
- Recovery: one small, concrete anchor in any zone. A call to an old friend (past), a walk with full attention (present), a small commitment to next week (future). Any one will do.

### The Counterweight Type: ⚖️ Balanced Time Perspective
Your wholeness lives in what you might hold. The Balanced profile's engagement across all three zones is a picture of what full temporal participation can look like. You do not have to maximize all three. But if the floating ever feels like flatness rather than freedom, the Balanced profile is a map of rooms you can choose to light.

### Your Reflection
"You are the one who moves through time lightly, unheld by any zone. If that lightness is freedom, tend it. If it ever feels like flatness, let someone help you look. Your gift is non-attachment, and your growth is choosing, sometimes, to be held."

## Temporal stance modifier (v0.23)

The v0.22 profile describes which temporal zones you engage (the three factors). v0.23 adds a **temporal-stance modifier** that describes your relationship to the passage of time itself, grounded in Carstensen (2021) on perceived time horizons.

Four stances, reported alongside the zone type:

- **Resource**: time is something to be used well. A Keeper-as-Resource preserves roots as a steward. A Wayfinder-as-Resource plans and builds.
- **Weight**: time is something you carry. Reframed as "a weight you carry with dignity." The affirming scope limit note carries the clinical caveat: sustained passage-as-burden may warrant attention that this assessment cannot provide.
- **Gift**: time is something you receive. A Witness-as-Gift is present to the moment as something given. A Balanced Time Perspective with gift stance experiences all three zones as received.
- **Mystery**: time is something you dwell in. The Wanderer with mystery stance floats in time as a contemplative, not a drifter.

The stance modifier changes the texture of the type without changing the zone factors. The four-stance typology is our synthesis and is unvalidated; the underlying perceived-time-horizons mechanism is validated (Carstensen). The stance layer is a forced-ranking or best-fit format, a departure from the zone factors' Likert format.

## How scores modulate these profiles

The base profile above is the type archetype. A player's exact three factor scores modulate it.

| Signal | Meaning | Effect on the profile |
|---|---|---|
| High prototypicality (engaged factors far above 60, others clearly below) | Strongly that type | Report the archetype at full strength. |
| Near a threshold (a factor within ±5 of 60) | Between types | Surface the dimensional scores and the near-miss; do not force a clean label. |
| Two patterns nearly tied (e.g., Past 64, Present 62, Future 30) | Soft fit, leaning between Hearth and Keeper | Report the assigned type (Hearth) with a note that the present engagement is near the threshold. |
| All three factors within ±5 of 60 | Between Balanced and others | Report the dimensional scores; if all three clear, Balanced; otherwise note the soft fit. |

There is no facet-tension modifier and no boundary condition. Independent factors do not cancel, and the multi-zone types are first-class results.

## Factor reporting (core, not optional)

The PTA reports three independent factor scores on every result: Past-Positive engagement, Present-eudaimonic engagement, Future engagement. The type is the interpretive label; the three scores are the measurement. A Keeper result reads "Past 78, Present 41, Future 35"; the type follows from that pattern. The factors are validated constructs (ZTPI Past-Positive and Future; Vowinckel present-eudaimonic), not designer composites; that line is stated on the results screen, along with the affirming-scope note that the negative-valence halves of each zone are not measured.
