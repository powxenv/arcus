                                                                                                                                                    energetic arousal and tense arousal are mixtures of valence and a single activation
                                                                                                                                                    dimension. The authors present a direct test of this hypothesis by computing the
                                                                                                                                                    correlation between the residuals of energetic arousal and tense arousal after re-
                                                                                                                                                    moving the shared variance with valence. Whereas the valence activation hypoth-

                                                                                                                                                    that it was not significantly different from 0. This finding reaffirms the view of
                                                                                                                                                    energetic arousal and tense arousal as 2 distinct types of activation.
