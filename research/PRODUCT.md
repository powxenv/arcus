# PRODUCT.md

## Register
Product (consumer app). Design serves the experience: personality assessments taken for self-discovery and play. Users are in a reflective, curious mood, not a task-completion flow.

## Target users
General consumers (18+) who enjoy personality tests and self-reflection. Not clinical, not academic. People who would take a Buzzfeed quiz but want something deeper, more grounded, and more beautiful.

## Product purpose
Arcus is a four-test personality suite that measures the trajectory of who you are, not just where you stand. Each test maps a different dimension (energy, mind, self, time) and reveals a direction. The suite payoff is the four arcs together.

## Brand personality
Warm, precise, curious, playful without being silly. Thinks like a researcher, talks like a friend. Never clinical, never generic.

## Anti-references
- MBTI and 16Personalities (binary boxes, false precision)
- Clinical assessment dashboards (cold, dense, clinical)
- Buzzfeed quizzes (flippant, shallow, disposable)
- Generic SaaS landing pages (corporate, forgettable)

## Strategic design principles
- Light, airy, colorful. Never dark, heavy, or corporate.
- One action at a time. The user is never asked to do two things at once.
- Direction over position. Every result emphasizes where you're going, not just where you are.
- Accessible by default. Contrast, keyboard, screen readers from the first line of code.
- Fun to take. The assessment itself should be enjoyable, not a chore to complete.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). Full keyboard navigation. Clear focus states. No motion without reduced-motion fallback. No color-only signaling.
